About

My best work often starts the same way. Someone shows me the messy, manual version of their job. The workaround they've been living with for months or years. I keep digging until I understand what's really going on, pull the data apart, and work with design and technical partners to turn that into something people will actually use.

The part I love is when a process that took hours gets down to minutes, and you can see the relief.

I spent most of my career doing this at T-Mobile, where I built and led T-Builder in store development. Now I'm exploring what's next and building with AI tools along the way. I write SQL, I partner with designers and engineers, and I'm learning to ship things myself using Claude and other AI development tools.

Proptech

Schedule Me

A scheduling board for move-in and move-out visits on single-family rentals. A requestor posts an address, date, time window, and checklist. A worker whose skills and availability fit can take the visit. The board tracks jobs across Not Started, In Progress, Completed, and Canceled. Built as a browser prototype with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Home

Homeowner Maintenance

Tracks recurring house work that usually lives in someone's head until something fails: HVAC filters, detectors, gutters, and similar tasks, each with a frequency and due date. The dashboard groups everything as overdue, due soon, or upcoming. You can export reminders to your calendar, share with another person, and assign tasks to yourself or each other. Defaults come from the EPA, HUD, and university extension checklists. Built with Next.js, TypeScript, React, and Supabase.
